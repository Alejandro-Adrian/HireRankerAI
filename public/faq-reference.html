🚀 Getting Started

Q: How do I create a new ranking?

Go to Rankings tab → Click 'Create New' → Select job position → Add criteria and weights → Review and save.

Q: How do I upload resumes?

Click 'Upload Resumes' → Drag & drop PDF/DOC files (max 5MB) → System auto-extracts candidate info → Review and save.

Q: What video platform is used?

We use VideoSDK for video calls. You don't need additional software—just a browser and webcam.

Q: What's the maximum file size for resumes?

5MB per file. Supported formats: PDF, DOC, DOCX. Larger files will be rejected.

⭐ Rankings & Scoring

Q: How is the final rank calculated?

Each criterion gets a score (1-10) × its weight → Total = sum of weighted scores → Candidates sorted automatically from highest to lowest.

Q: How do I score applications?

Open ranking → View applications → Click score icon → Rate each candidate against your criteria → AI calculates total score.

Q: Can I edit a ranking after creation?

Yes, click the ranking → Edit tab → Modify criteria/weights → Save changes. Re-scoring is recommended after edits.

Q: How do I filter applications by status?

Rankings tab → Click filter icon → Select status (New, Reviewing, Approved, Rejected) → Apply filter to view subset.

🎥 Video Interviews

Q: How do I schedule an interview?

Select candidate → Click 'Schedule Interview' → Choose date/time → Add interview type/notes → Send invitation.

Q: How do video interviews work?

Schedule interview → Candidate receives link → Join call → AI records & transcribes conversation → Get auto-generated summary.

Q: How long does transcription take?

Usually 2-5 minutes after interview ends. You'll get a notification when transcription is ready to view.

Q: Can I host multiple interviews simultaneously?

Yes! Create separate video sessions. You can manage them independently in your dashboard.

Q: How do I resend an interview invitation?

Open video session → Click candidate name → 'Resend Invitation' → Email sent immediately to candidate.

Q: Can I see if a candidate accepted the interview?

Check video session details → See RSVP status → Green = accepted, Gray = pending, Red = declined.

Q: What if audio doesn't record?

Check microphone permissions in browser settings → Refresh page → Try again. Contact support if issue persists.

⚡ Bulk Operations

Q: How do I invite multiple candidates?

Select ranking → Use checkboxes to select multiple candidates → Click 'Bulk Invite' → Confirm and invitations sent.

Q: Can I export results?

Yes! In Results tab → Click 'Export' → Choose CSV or PDF format → Download ranking with all scores and candidate data.

Q: How do I delete a ranking or application?

Open ranking/application → Click 3-dot menu → Select 'Delete' → Confirm action. Note: This action is irreversible.

Q: Can I undo a decision (approve/reject)?

No, approval/rejection is final. You'll need to recreate the ranking to change a candidate's status.

👤 Account Management

Q: How do I reset my password?

Click 'Forgot Password' on login → Enter email → Verify with code sent to inbox → Create new password → Sign in.

Q: How do I change my profile settings?

Click your avatar → Settings → Update name, email, password, or preferences → Save changes to apply.

Q: Can I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Scroll to 'Delete Account' → Confirm with verification code → Account and all data removed (cannot restore).

📊 Analytics & Reports

Q: Can I see interview analytics?

Yes! Analytics tab shows rankings performance, completion rates, time data, scoring trends, and candidate insights.

Q: How do I share rankings with my team?

Currently for individual use. Contact support for team collaboration features and multi-user access.

Q: Can I generate reports?

Yes! Go to Results tab → Select ranking → Click 'Generate Report' → PDF created with scores, notes, and recommendations.
